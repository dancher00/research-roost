# /internship — research internships and visiting placements

Use this workflow for research internships, research placements and visiting-student opportunities across the sciences, humanities, social sciences, medicine, engineering and arts/practice research. Read `docs/opportunities.md`, `docs/sources.md` and `docs/rubric.md`. A generic software internship is not automatically a research placement.

1. Load the confirmed profile and selected private workspace. Establish the research question, skills to develop, placement timing and any supplied host URL. Preserve undecided countries and funding preferences. Record a selected research-internship mode as `preferences.opportunity_types: ["research_internship"]` only when the user selects it; exploring `./roost search-plan --family internship` does not itself change preferences.
2. Run the family search plan and use the active agent's search/browser tools. Inspect official host, project, archive, museum, research institute, university and employer pages. Use configured public sources where suitable; built-in doctoral boards do not guarantee internship coverage. Report exact sources, query mode, retrieved sample, blocked sources and search limits.
3. Archive the complete original advert with `opportunity add URL` or an accurately identified manual import. Use `research_internship` only with source support. Check the applicable cycle, host and reference. The same reusable programme URL can represent several intakes: pass `--edition LABEL` or `--session LABEL` on add/import to keep separate records while preserving the original URL. A label is not evidence the next cycle is open.
4. Verify eligibility from the actual advert: permitted/excluded degree levels, current enrollment, enrollment throughout the placement, required return to study, minimum study stage, work authorization, home-institution approval and any exact citizenship/residency restrictions. Compare source-linked candidate evidence only. A degree in progress alone does not establish enrollment through the advertised end date. Missing evidence remains unknown.
5. Record actual start/end dates, fixed/flexible duration and required availability. Separate application, reference, funding, acceptance and onboarding deadlines, preserving exact timezones or date-only precision. Check `application_status`; a future date or old evergreen page does not prove live recruitment. Candidate availability must be confirmed, not guessed from a CV.
6. Follow `/funding`. Separate paid/unpaid, stipend/salary basis and period, registration/tuition where applicable, living, accommodation and travel support. Competitive support is conditional. Do not call an hourly/daily payment a fully funded scholarship or assume visas and relocation are covered.
7. Follow `/supervisor` for the actual mentor/project group and primary works where relevant. Read the advertised project, methods/materials, resources and feasible contribution during the placement. No dedicated Scholar/GitHub connector is implied: the active agent archives and assesses the sources it actually inspected.
8. Run `./roost screen ID`, generate the family-bound assessment packet and complete project_fit, methods, mentor_fit, preparedness, funding and environment. Use exact posting/external evidence and candidate pointers. Rank within the internship comparison group; keep missing mentor availability and other unknowns explicit.
9. Follow `/apply` with only requested documents: CV, project-specific motivation, research statement/portfolio, portal answers, enrollment evidence or references as required. Draft realistic contributions and confirmed availability. Review all claims, render where requested and verify the final packet. No email, application or referee request is sent automatically.
10. Deliver an internship shortlist with original sources, dates, eligibility state, funding components, research rationale, gaps and next actions. Regenerate the private report/calendar when useful. Use `/outcome` only for actual user-confirmed submissions and results; do not describe a prepared packet as a submitted placement application.
