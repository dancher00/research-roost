# /funding — funding, fees, eligibility and financial feasibility

Use original university, doctoral school, internship host, school organiser, scholarship/grant and official policy sources. Read `docs/opportunities.md`. Rules and amounts change: verify the applicable cycle/date before using them. Board labels are discovery hints.

1. Establish family and route: doctoral employee contract/studentship, paid or unpaid research internship, or taught/research/field school with registration costs. Separate admission from funding and guaranteed coverage from a competitive scholarship needing another application. A tuition condition may be inapplicable to an internship or school; record not_applicable with evidence rather than claiming a tuition waiver.
2. Quote funding amount, currency, gross/net or unspecified basis, period, duration, progression/renewal conditions, start date, employment/student status and expected work/teaching. Do not convert gross salary into disposable income or compare incomparable currencies/periods without an explicit, dated assumption.
3. Verify tuition coverage for the candidate's applicable fee category, mandatory institutional/registration fees, insurance, research/bench/fieldwork costs, travel/relocation and language/visa/application fees where applicable. An advertised stipend or salary is not proof all these are covered. Keep estimates separate from official amounts and explain unknowns that could change affordability.
4. Quote restrictions verbatim: citizenship/residency/fee status, degree/equivalence, prior doctoral enrolment/degree, research experience, language/test validity, country or institution restrictions, mobility window, compatible funding and external sponsorship. Ask for sensitive profile facts only when a specific rule requires them. Do not infer nationality from name/location or eligibility from nationality alone.
5. For mobility rules, identify the exact reference date, lookback period, maximum residence/activity, exclusions and official wording. Compare dated residence/activity history only when complete enough; uncertain boundary-month counting stays unresolved. This is source-based screening, not an institutional eligibility determination.
6. Record confirmed metadata with `opportunity annotate`; use the exact quote and current snapshot digest. `funding.status` is fully_funded, partial, self_funded or unknown. Separate `tuition_covered`, amount, currency, period and basis. `funding.components` records tuition, registration, living, accommodation and travel separately, each as covered, partial, not_covered, not_applicable or unknown with conditions and sourced amounts. A fee waiver is not living/travel support; daily/week payments are not monthly salaries. Record separate scholarship and admission/registration cutoffs in `deadlines`. Add a funding dossier for separate official sources and a dated cost scenario when requested.
7. Run `./roost screen ID`. Full-funding preferences require explicit support and tuition coverage. Minimum funding compares only equal currencies, periods and basis. Unknown does not become fail or pass merely to finish a shortlist. Any conditions the deterministic engine cannot interpret remain named questions.
8. Present official coverage, candidate-specific eligibility, financial scenario assumptions, material unknowns, separate deadlines and the right official contact. Prepare a concise question for unresolved points if requested; never send it automatically.
