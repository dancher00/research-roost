# /school — summer, winter, research, training and field schools

Use this workflow for summer schools, winter schools, research/training/field schools and research-relevant short courses in any discipline. Read `docs/opportunities.md`, `docs/sources.md` and `docs/rubric.md`. Summer and winter are seasons within the school family; school attendance is distinct from admission to a doctoral degree or employment in a research internship.

1. Load the confirmed profile. Establish the desired learning outcome, actual current preparation and available dates if the user has supplied them. Do not assume the user is a PhD student, needs publications or wants only AI schools. Record selected exact types in `preferences.opportunity_types` when confirmed; otherwise use `./roost search-plan --family school` as a one-off exploration without altering preferences.
2. Use the plan with the active agent's search/browser tools. Search official universities, societies, research centres, institutes, archives, museums and school organisers. Curated lists are discovery leads; verify the original organiser's current edition. State geography/discipline coverage, queried sources, source failures and the limits of the search.
3. Archive the complete programme/announcement using a direct URL or identified manual import. Preserve the original URL. Reused annual URLs need distinct `--edition LABEL` or `--session LABEL` identities on add/import for different years or sessions; do not invent a tracking query to force deduplication apart. A last year's page is not a verified next-year event. Confirm title, organiser, location/format, edition and open/upcoming/closed/rolling/unknown application status.
4. Record actual event start/end dates, contact days or duration, online/hybrid/in-person format, language, syllabus, instructors, exercises and expected outputs. Keep application, scholarship/travel-grant, early registration, regular registration, poster/abstract and acceptance deadlines as separate named entries. An open registration window does not imply the scholarship window is open. Preserve exact cutoff precision and timezones.
5. Verify actual prerequisites and degree/enrollment restrictions: background knowledge, language, software/access/materials, required study stage, nomination or home-institution approval. A school may admit undergraduates, graduates, researchers or practitioners. Do not substitute a doctorate requirement or judge a humanities field school by publication counts.
6. Follow `/funding` and record tuition/registration, living, accommodation and travel separately. Mark not_applicable only when justified. A free registration fee is not a fully covered trip; fee waivers and travel grants may have separate competitive applications, restrictions and earlier deadlines. Quote amounts, currency, period/basis, what is covered and conditions. Keep estimated travel budgets distinct from official support.
7. Evaluate learning fit from the actual syllabus and candidate evidence: which specific method/question the school advances, whether prerequisites are met, whether training depth matches the user's needs, and a realistic subsequent research use. Inspect instructors' relevant works only where they bear on programme content; no supervisor availability or doctoral supervision is implied by teaching a session.
8. Run `./roost screen ID`, generate the school-bound assessment packet and complete learning_fit, prerequisites, programme_fit, funding and practical_fit. Use exact evidence and profile pointers; unknown is not an average score. Rank schools within their own comparison group, separate from internships and PhD routes.
9. Follow `/apply` for the actual prompts: CV, learning-goal motivation, abstract/poster, prerequisites statement, references or funding request only when required. Use portal_answers for specific fields and attach genuine supporting documents. Never require a PhD proposal by default. Complete substantive drafting, critical review, rendering when needed and final verification.
10. Deliver an evidence-backed shortlist and calendar/report with event dates, distinct deadlines, learning benefit, cost coverage, eligibility questions and next actions. Do not register, pay, submit an abstract, contact organisers or publish personal reports without explicit user instruction. Record actual submission/registration or outcomes separately after confirmation.
