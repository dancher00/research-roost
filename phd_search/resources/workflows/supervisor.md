# /supervisor — supervisor, lab and programme research

The deliverable is an evidence-backed dossier and a focused research-fit conclusion, not a reputation ranking. Use the user's intended topic and disciplinary context. For internships examine the actual mentor/project; for schools examine organisers, instructors and syllabus only where relevant. A teaching role does not establish doctoral supervision, and schools need no named PI to be useful.

1. Identify the exact person/group/programme from an official institution page. Disambiguate names and affiliations. Create `./roost dossier create --name NAME --kind supervisor` (or lab/programme). Fetch explicit public source URLs using `dossier fetch ID URL`; sources retain exact text, dates and digests.
2. Establish current affiliation, research questions and methods, doctoral route, stated vacancies and contact instructions. Use official group/department/admissions/grant pages. An old vacancy or a faculty listing does not establish current recruiting, funded supervision or capacity.
3. Inspect a small, relevant set of primary outputs: papers, books/chapters, editions, designs, exhibitions, software/data, field/clinical outputs as appropriate. Verify names, dates, publication status and identifiers. Distinguish an agent's inspection of a work from a candidate claim to have read it. Record what was actually examined and its coverage.
4. Compare the intellectual agenda to confirmed candidate evidence: a shared question, complementary method, transferable contribution and specific learning needs. Describe fit in concrete terms; do not use institution rank, h-index or publication volume as a substitute for mentoring fit.
5. Review observable environment facts: supervision structure, training, resources, archives/fieldwork/equipment access, doctoral school, collaborators, teaching expectations and published funding terms. Mentoring quality, internal culture, working hours, availability and willingness to supervise remain unknown unless directly evidenced.
6. Populate `dossier.md` with a fact/source/excerpt/date table, concise synthesis, uncertainties and next questions. Attach the dossier to the relevant opportunity assessment through archived external-evidence references. Do not alter eligibility from unsourced impressions.
7. Draft a short personalised inquiry when requested. Identify the actual project or question, one relevant evidenced achievement, a specific connection to the person's work, and one answerable question. Respect no-contact instructions and programme application routes. Never imply prior contact, agreed supervision, guaranteed funding or a paper-read claim without evidence.
8. Deliver the dossier and draft locally. Sending an email or attaching personal material requires explicit instruction identifying the recipient and content. Creating a draft alone never changes contact/submission status.
